# Chronicle backends

`greentic-dw-memory-chronicle` (long-term memory) and
`greentic-dw-knowledge-chronicle` (document RAG) used to live in this workspace
under `memory/long-term/chronicle` and `knowledge/chronicle`. They depend on the
`chronicle-*` crates, which are only reachable as git dependencies of
`greentic-biz/greentic-chronicle-ext`. `cargo publish` refuses a git-only
dependency even when it is optional, so while they lived here no crate in this
workspace could be published.

Since the move to the single `1.2.0-dev` crates.io lane (2026-09-23) both crates
live in `greentic-biz/greentic-chronicle-ext` under `crates/`, with
`publish = false`. They depend on this workspace's contract crates
(`greentic-dw-memory-long-term`, `greentic-dw-knowledge`, `greentic-dw-llm`,
`greentic-dw-embedding`) by crates.io version range.

What stays here, deliberately:

- `KnowledgeVariant::Chronicle`, `LongTermMemoryVariant::Chronicle` and the
  catalog / provider-type ids that name them — they are strings and enums, not a
  dependency on chronicle code.
- The `long-term-chronicle` and `knowledge/chronicle` entries in
  `packs/gtpacks.manifest.json`. Those gtpacks are scaffolds generated by
  `gtc wizard` from the manifest's `category` / `name` / `pack_id` alone; they do
  not compile either crate.

A consumer that wants a Chronicle backend (greentic-runner-host) takes the
contract crates from crates.io and the backend crate from
greentic-chronicle-ext.
